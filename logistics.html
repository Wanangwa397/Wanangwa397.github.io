Logistics Operations Analysis

A Power BI dashboard focused on shipment performance, delivery delays, fleet utilization, high-volume locations, delay causes, and operational weather conditions.

Power BI DAX Power Query Data Modeling Logistics Analytics Data Visualization

Project Overview

This project uses Power BI to analyze logistics operations across shipment activity, delivery performance, fleet usage, operational locations, and delay causes. The overview dashboard provides a concise view of total shipments, on-time delivery, delayed shipments, fleet utilization, shipment status, top locations by volume, and delay reasons.

Key Analysis Areas

The analysis focuses on shipment status trends throughout the year, fleet utilization across trucks, geographic shipment volume, and the main contributors to delays such as traffic, mechanical failure, and weather. A separate weather monitoring page tracks temperature and humidity alongside operational measures such as waiting time, inventory level, and transaction amount.
