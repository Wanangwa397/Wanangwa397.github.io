Hospital Patient Metrics

A Power BI healthcare analytics dashboard focused on patient volume, admission performance, wait times, satisfaction, demographics, and department referral patterns.

Power BI DAX Power Query Healthcare Analytics Data Visualization

Project Overview

This project uses Power BI to summarize key hospital patient metrics across the 2021–2023 period. The dashboard provides a high-level view of patient volume, admission rate, average waiting time, patient satisfaction, demographic distribution, and referral activity.

Key Analysis Areas

The dashboard tracks total patients, admitted versus not admitted patients, average wait time, and average satisfaction. It also examines patient volume by race, gender, age band, day of the week, and department referral to support a broader view of hospital activity.
