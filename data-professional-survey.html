Data Professional Survey Analysis

A Power BI dashboard analyzing survey responses from data professionals, including job roles, salaries, programming language preferences, work-life balance, salary satisfaction, and career-entry difficulty.

Power BI Power Query Data Visualization Survey Analysis Business Intelligence

Project Overview

This project uses Power BI to analyze survey data from data professionals and provide an overview of their roles, salaries, programming preferences, geographic distribution, and overall job satisfaction.

Key Analysis Areas

The dashboard examines average salary by job title, country of residence, favorite programming languages, difficulty entering the data profession, work-life balance satisfaction, salary satisfaction, and respondent demographics.
