University Institutional Research Overview

An interactive executive dashboard that brings together enrollment, academic performance, first-year retention, six-year graduation, and financial-aid indicators for institutional decision-making.

View on Tableau Public → Explore Dashboard

Project Overview

This project uses synthetic university data to demonstrate how an institutional research team can monitor student outcomes from one concise dashboard. The layout prioritizes high-level KPIs while retaining enough detail to compare enrollment and graduation outcomes across academic years, terms, and colleges.

Dashboard Features

  • Executive KPIs for enrollment, GPA, retention, graduation, and aid coverage
  • Enrollment trends by academic year and term
  • College-level enrollment and graduation comparisons
  • Click-to-filter dashboard actions
  • Cross-chart college highlighting on hover
