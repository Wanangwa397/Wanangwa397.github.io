Project Overview

This Power BI project analyzes Superstore business performance across sales, profitability, returned orders, products, customer segments, geographic markets, and time.

The report compares current performance with the previous year to identify growth, profitability trends, operational improvements, and areas where product performance may require attention.

Overall Performance: Sales and profit both increased substantially compared with the previous year. Profit grew slightly faster than sales, while the returned-order rate declined. Together, these indicators suggest that business growth was accompanied by improved operational performance rather than being driven by revenue growth alone.

Key Business Insights

Strong Year-over-Year Growth

Sales increased from $1.58M in the previous year to $2.33M, representing growth of 47.16%. Profit increased from $196.37K to $292.30K, an improvement of 48.85%. Profit therefore grew slightly faster than revenue, indicating that the additional sales did not significantly weaken overall profitability.

Returned Orders Improved

Returned orders decreased from 8.74% in the previous year to 5.79%. This represents an improvement of 2.95 percentage points and approximately a one-third reduction relative to the previous return rate. Lower returns may help protect both revenue and operational efficiency.

Product Profitability Is Uneven

Profit varies considerably across product groups. Copiers are the strongest profit contributor at approximately $56.09K, while Tables are the largest loss-making product group at approximately -$17.75K. Bookcases and Supplies also appear slightly unprofitable, highlighting potential opportunities to investigate pricing, discounting, or product costs.

Technology Is a Major Profit Driver

Several Technology products are among the strongest contributors to profit. Copiers generate the highest product profit, while Phones and Accessories also make substantial positive contributions. Technology therefore represents an important category for protecting profitability and supporting future growth.

Sales Are Concentrated by Segment

The largest customer segment contributes 50.3% of total sales. The remaining segments contribute 30.8% and 18.9%. Because approximately half of total revenue comes from one segment, maintaining performance within that customer group is particularly important to overall sales results.

Sales Show Growth Despite Volatility

Monthly sales fluctuate throughout the reporting period, including several pronounced peaks. However, later periods generally reach higher sales levels than earlier periods, and the current sales series frequently exceeds the previous-year comparison. This pattern supports the strong year-over-year growth reported by the headline sales KPI.

Business Recommendations

  • Investigate the drivers of losses within Tables and other weak-performing product groups, including pricing, discounting, shipping costs, and product-level margins.
  • Protect and expand high-performing Technology products such as Copiers, Phones, and Accessories.
  • Continue monitoring returned orders to determine whether the improvement from 8.74% to 5.79% can be sustained.
  • Examine the largest customer segment in greater detail because it represents approximately half of total sales.
  • Investigate periods of unusually high or low monthly sales to identify seasonal patterns and potential opportunities for sales planning.
  • Compare product profitability across states and customer segments to determine whether weak products perform differently across markets.

Interactive Report Features
