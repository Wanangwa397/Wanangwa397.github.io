University Institutional Research Dashboard

A comprehensive Power BI solution analyzing university admissions, enrollment, retention, graduation, academic performance, financial aid, and course outcomes.

Power BI DAX Power Query Data Modeling Data Validation Institutional Research

Project Overview

This project uses Power BI to analyze a synthetic university dataset containing 25,000 students and more than 470,000 course registration records. It brings together institutional data covering admissions, enrollment, academic performance, financial aid, graduation, students, programs, courses, faculty, countries, dates, and academic terms.

The institutional overview presents key indicators such as enrolled students, yield rate, first-year retention rate, six-year graduation rate, average term GPA, and financial aid coverage. These measures provide a concise view of institutional performance and student outcomes.

Data Model and Validation

The dashboard is supported by a relational data model connecting admissions, enrollment, academic performance, course registration, financial aid, and graduation fact tables with student, program, course, faculty, country, date, and term dimensions. Hierarchies were created to support analysis by academic year, term, college, department, program, course level, region, and country.

Data quality checks were performed before reporting to identify and correct invalid dates, missing values, inconsistent categories, zero-credit courses, and unmatched student records. Thirty-three validation tests were completed successfully to confirm the accuracy and consistency of the final dataset.

Key Analysis Areas

The analysis examines applicant conversion, admission and yield rates, enrollment trends, student demographics, academic performance, course outcomes, financial aid distribution, first-year retention, and four-year and six-year graduation rates. Results can be compared across colleges, departments, programs, cohorts, residency groups, and other student populations.

Dedicated dashboard pages allow institutional leaders and analysts to identify performance trends, compare student outcomes, evaluate differences between academic units, and explore factors associated with persistence and degree completion.
