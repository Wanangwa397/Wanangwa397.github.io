Key Business Insights

1. Strong Year-over-Year Growth

Sales increased from $1.58M to $2.33M, representing growth of 47.16%. Profit increased from $196.37K to $292.30K, or 48.85%. Profit growth slightly exceeded sales growth, showing that profitability remained strong as revenue expanded.

2. Returns Improved Materially

The returned-order rate decreased from 8.74% to 5.79%, an improvement of 2.95 percentage points. This represents approximately a one-third reduction relative to the previous-year return rate.

3. Product Profitability Is Uneven

Copiers are the strongest profit contributor at approximately $56.09K. Tables are the largest loss-making product group at approximately -$17.75K. Bookcases and Supplies also appear slightly unprofitable, suggesting that pricing, discounting, and product costs should be reviewed.

4. Technology Drives Profit

Technology contains several of the strongest profit contributors, including Copiers, Phones, and Accessories. These products represent an important source of profitability for the business.

5. Sales Are Concentrated by Segment

The largest customer segment accounts for 50.3% of sales, while the remaining two segments account for 30.8% and 18.9%. This concentration makes the largest segment particularly important to overall revenue performance.

6. Sales Trend Is Positive but Volatile

Monthly sales fluctuate throughout the reporting period, but later periods show repeated high points. The current sales series also frequently exceeds the previous-year comparison, supporting the strong overall year-over-year growth.

Business Recommendations

  • Investigate the losses associated with Tables and other weak-performing products, particularly pricing, discounts, and product-level costs.
  • Protect and expand strong Technology product lines, particularly Copiers, Phones, and Accessories.
  • Continue monitoring returned orders to determine whether the improvement from 8.74% to 5.79% can be sustained.
  • Analyze the largest customer segment more closely because it represents approximately half of total sales.
  • Investigate monthly sales peaks and declines to identify seasonal patterns and improve sales planning.
