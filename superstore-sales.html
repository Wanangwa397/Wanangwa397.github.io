Project Overview

This Power BI project analyzes sales performance across multiple areas of the business. The dashboard provides a consolidated view of revenue, orders, customers, product performance, sales channels, geographic trends, delivery performance, and product returns.

The goal of the dashboard is to make sales information easier to explore and help identify patterns that can support better business decisions.

Key Analysis Areas

Sales Performance

Tracks overall sales activity and provides a clear view of business performance across the reporting period.

Customer Analysis

Examines customer activity to understand purchasing patterns and customer contribution to overall sales.

Product Performance

Compares product performance to identify stronger and weaker areas of the product portfolio.

Sales Channels

Evaluates performance across different sales channels to understand where revenue is being generated.

Geographic Performance

Provides a geographic view of sales to identify differences in performance across locations.

Courier Performance

Reviews courier and delivery performance to identify operational patterns that may affect customer experience.

Product Returns

Analyzes returned products to help identify areas that may require further investigation.

Business Value

Dashboard purpose: The report brings sales, customer, product, geographic, delivery, and return information together in one place, allowing users to quickly compare performance and identify areas that may require additional attention.

Report Features

  • Interactive Power BI visualizations for exploring sales performance.
  • Product and customer performance analysis.
  • Sales channel and geographic comparisons.
  • Courier and delivery performance analysis.
  • Product return analysis.
  • Business-focused dashboard design for quick interpretation.
