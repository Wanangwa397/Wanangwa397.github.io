Project Overview

This Power BI project analyzes Superstore sales performance and profitability across products, customer segments, geographic locations, and time.

The dashboard compares current performance with the previous year while also tracking returned orders and identifying differences in profitability across product groups.

Key Business Insights

Strong Year-over-Year Growth

Sales increased from $1.58M to $2.33M, representing growth of 47.16%. Profit also increased from $196.37K to $292.30K, a 48.85% improvement.

Returned Orders Improved

Returned orders decreased from 8.74% in the previous year to 5.79%, showing an improvement in overall order retention.

Product Profitability Varies

Profit differs substantially across products. Copiers are among the strongest profit contributors, while Tables show the largest negative profitability.

Technology Performs Strongly

Technology products such as Copiers, Accessories, and Phones generate some of the strongest profits shown in the dashboard.

Sales Differ by Customer Segment

The dashboard shows that the largest customer segment represents 50.3% of sales, followed by segments contributing 30.8% and 18.9%.

Geographic Profitability

The state-level map highlights differences in profitability across the United States, allowing higher- and lower-performing geographic markets to be identified.

Dashboard Analysis

  • Sales compared with the previous year over time.
  • Profit performance by individual product.
  • Geographic profit analysis by state.
  • Sales distribution across customer segments.
  • Current-year and previous-year KPI comparisons.
  • Returned-order performance monitoring.

Business Value

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of sales and profitability performance, making it easier to identify profitable products, weaker product areas, geographic differences, customer segment contribution, and changes in performance over time.

These insights can support product strategy, sales planning, customer analysis, and continued monitoring of returned orders.
