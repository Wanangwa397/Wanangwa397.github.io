Superstore Sales Analytics

A Power BI sales dashboard analyzing sales, profit, returned orders, product profitability, geographic performance, customer segments, and year-over-year trends.

Power BI DAX Power Query Data Modeling Data Visualization

Project Overview

This project uses Power BI to provide a high-level overview of Superstore sales and profitability. The dashboard summarizes key business metrics including total sales, total profit, returned orders, and year-over-year performance.

Key Analysis Areas

The dashboard examines sales trends over time, product profitability, geographic performance by state, customer segment sales, returned orders, and comparisons with previous-year performance.
